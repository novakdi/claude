# Literature digest – 2026-10-26

## 1. Niche-level immune evasion in TP53-mutant AML residual disease revealed by spatial proteomics (bioRxiv preprint, May 2026)
PMID 42239259 · DOI 10.64898/2026.05.15.725421 — *closest to your project (multiplexed imaging of AML marrow)*
**Summary:** Sequential immunofluorescence on whole BM biopsies from TP53-wild-type and TP53-mutant AML at diagnosis and in complete remission (CR), mapping hematopoietic and stromal/immune niches around MRD cells.
**Main findings:**
- Mutant leukemia cells identified by high p53; B-cell depletion and increased Tregs in mutant BM at CR.
- Persistent erythroid/immature leukemia clusters in perisinusoidal niches, spatially segregated from T-cell clusters (niche-level immune evasion).
- FOXP3-high Tregs enriched near MRD cells.
- scRNA-seq cell-cell communication suggests erythroid–T-cell GDF15–CD48 axis as a suppression mechanism.

## 2. Mitochondria transfer via tunneling nanotubes drives macrophage immunosuppression in pediatric AML (Leukemia, Oct 2026)
PMID 42855473 · DOI 10.1038/s41375-026-03164-1 — *pediatric AML*
**Summary:** Princess Máxima Center study on how AML cells reprogram macrophages.
**Main findings:**
- Immunosuppressive macrophages dominate the microenvironment in pediatric AML (RUNX1::RUNX1T1, KMT2A-r).
- AML cells switch macrophages to an anti-inflammatory state (NF-κB suppression) by direct contact and TNT-mediated mitochondrial transfer, with OXPHOS rewiring.
- Reprogrammed macrophages: impaired phagocytosis, chemoresistance, reduced T-cell and bispecific-antibody killing.

## 3. ROS define leukemia stem cell identity in pediatric AML (Cancer Lett, Oct 2026)
PMID 42830144 · DOI 10.1016/j.canlet.2026.218866 — *pediatric AML*
**Summary:** Uses intracellular ROS levels to isolate functional LSCs from pediatric AML.
**Main findings:**
- ROS-low cells are quiescent, serially replate, engraft in mice, localize to hypoxic regions in a 3D niche model and resist cytarabine.
- Stemness/niche-adhesion programs up, OXPHOS down, yet complex I inhibition selectively kills them.
- ROS-low signature identifies LSCs in pediatric scRNA-seq data and is prognostic.

## 4. Latexin deletion in the BM niche suppresses leukemia via LIFR downregulation and immune activation (JCI Insight, Oct 2026)
PMID 42847991 · DOI 10.1172/jci.insight.199771 — *murine MLL-AF9 AML niche model*
**Main findings:**
- Lxn-KO recipients had longer survival, fewer LSCs and blasts.
- Stromal scRNA-seq: reduced LIFR signaling in MSCs → higher Cxcl9 → more CD8 T-cell recruitment and cytotoxicity.
- Combined with anti-PD-1 further prolonged survival.

## 5. BM MSCs are functionally remodeled after complete remission of AML (J Transl Int Med, Aug 2026)
PMID 42657369 · DOI 10.1515/jtim-2026-0048
**Main findings:**
- Dysfunctional AML MSCs (differentiation, apoptosis, cytokines, regulatory function) return near healthy-control state in CR; fewer senescent MSCs.
- CR MSCs can be re-induced to senescence by AML cells; confirmed in an AML-CR mouse model.
- Adult cohort; relevant to niche recovery after therapy.

## 6. Inflammatory Signatures in MDS (Cells, Jun 2026; narrative review)
PMID 42439614 · DOI 10.3390/cells15131137 — *MDS*
**Main findings:**
- Chronic inflammation/inflammaging actively drives clonal selection and niche remodeling.
- TP53 and spliceosome mutations amplify inflammatory signaling and reshape the niche.
- Peripheral blood inflammatory indices correlate with prognosis and therapy response.

## 7. CellFuse: multimodal integration of single-cell and spatial proteomics data (Cancer Res, Jul 2026)
PMID 42084223 · DOI 10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-3699 — *methods, CODEX-type data*
**Main findings:**
- Supervised contrastive learning framework integrating cytometry, CITE-seq and spatial proteomics despite sparse shared markers.
- Accurate cross-modality label transfer; outperforms existing methods in PB, BM and lymphoma, including rare subsets.
- In solid tumors reconstructs spatial microenvironments whose interactions correlate with treatment response.

## 8. Single-cell and multi-omics profiling of the multiple myeloma immune niche (Cancers, Sep 2026; review)
PMID 42882852 · DOI 10.3390/cancers18193158
**Main findings:**
- Synthesizes spatial transcriptomics and multiplexed imaging showing immune exclusion, stromal support and metabolic niches in myeloma marrow.
- Frames CAR-T/T-cell-engager response and resistance as dependent on spatial accessibility, antigen retention and myeloid suppression.
- Proposes a spatial-temporal framework for progression and relapse.
