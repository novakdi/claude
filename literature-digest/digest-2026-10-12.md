# Literature digest — 2026-10-12
Source: PubMed (searches for CODEX/multiplex imaging, pediatric AML/MDS, BM niche). No new CODEX-specific leukemia paper this week; closest are spatial/multiplex-IF studies in MDS and AML.

## Spatial / imaging (most relevant to CODEX)
1. **Spatial remodeling of BM architecture defines tissue-state signatures of disease activity and therapeutic response in MDS** — Nachman et al., Leukemia 2026. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41375-026-03029-7
   - Whole-slide multiplex IF + single-cell phenotyping; >5M cells; diagnostic (n=36), longitudinal (n=29), precursor (n=13), normal (n=21) biopsies.
   - MDS shows genotype-imprinted remodeling: altered progenitor patterning, disrupted erythroid islands, HSPC displaced from perivascular niches.
   - 82-feature Microarchitectural Perturbation Score (MDS-MAPS) separated remission vs active disease better than blast % (AUC 0.883 vs 0.660) and low-blast MDS vs CCUS (AUC 0.815); normalizes in remission, re-emerges at relapse. A template for linking spatial features to outcome/response.
2. **Niche-level immune evasion in TP53-mutant AML residual disease revealed by spatial proteomics** — Mizuno ... Andreeff, bioRxiv preprint 2026 (not peer reviewed). https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.15.725421
   - Sequential immunofluorescence on whole BM biopsies, diagnosis vs morphological CR; TP53-mutant cells identified by high p53.
   - TP53-mut CR marrow: B-cell depletion, more Tregs; persistent erythroid/immature leukemia clusters in perisinusoidal niches, spatially segregated from T-cell clusters; FOXP3+ Tregs enriched near MRD cells.
   - scRNA-seq suggests erythroid–T-cell GDF15–CD48 axis as suppression mechanism.
3. **Prefibrotic BM microenvironment is a hallmark of clonal hematopoiesis** — Aguilar-Navarro ... Tikhonova, Nat Immunol 2026. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-026-02668-3
   - scRNA-seq + histology + spatial transcriptomics of human CHIP marrow.
   - Fibro-inflammatory remodeling: expanded CAF-like fibroblasts, increased collagen/ECM programs; spatial fiber-enriched hubs with CXCL12+ fibroblasts, macrophages, lymphoid cells and TGF-β/TNF signaling — niche changes precede overt malignancy.

## Pediatric AML / MDS
4. **Mitochondria transfer via tunneling nanotubes drives macrophage immunosuppression in pediatric AML** — Perzolli ... Heidenreich, Leukemia 2026. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41375-026-03164-1
   - Immunosuppressive macrophages dominate pediatric AML marrow (RUNX1::RUNX1T1, KMT2A-r).
   - AML cells reprogram macrophages (NF-κB suppression) via contact-dependent TNT transfer of mitochondria → OXPHOS rewiring, impaired phagocytosis, chemoresistance, reduced T-cell/bispecific killing.
5. **A three-gene interferon signature predicts sustained CR in pediatric AML** — Sherif ... Deola, Cancers 2026. https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers18091423
   - 32 pediatric AML at diagnosis (NanoString IO360, RNA-seq, flow), validated in TARGET.
   - IFN signature tracks chemosensitivity, lower MRD, longer OS (also within standard-risk); linked to non-exhausted CD4/CD8 cytotoxic and CD8 TEMRA cells.
6. **ROS define leukemia stem cell identity in pediatric AML** — Benetton ... Pigazzi, Cancer Lett 2026. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.canlet.2026.218866
   - ROS-low cells are quiescent, engraft, localize to hypoxic regions in a 3D niche model, resist cytarabine; show niche-interaction programs yet depend on OXPHOS (complex I inhibition kills them).
   - RL-derived signature identifies LSCs in pediatric scRNA-seq data and is prognostic.
7. **Menin inhibition in pediatric UBTF-TD MDS** — Rummel ... Borkhardt, Mol Cell Pediatr 2026 (single case report). https://doi.org/10.1186/s40348-026-00264-x
   - UBTF-TD = high-risk pediatric MDS/AML subtype with HOX/MEIS1 program. Child with MDS-EB: revumenib added to aza/ven → transfusion independence, blast and UBTF-TD clearance, then alloHSCT, CR at 1 year. Hypothesis-generating only.

## Murine niche / functional validation (AML)
8. **FGF18 mediates fibroblast–leukemia cross talk in AML** — Zhang ... Xie, Blood 2026. https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026033125
   - Stromal fibroblast Fgf18 up in AML niche; FGF18→FGFR3→AKT-mTOR→IL-6 feed-forward loop (IL-6 also drives fibroblast JAK-STAT3/FGF18), suppressing CD8 T cells. Fibroblast Fgf18 loss delays disease; neutralizing antibody synergizes with anti-PD-1. High FGF18 correlates with poor prognosis.
9. **Latexin deletion in the BM niche suppresses leukemia via LIFR downregulation and immune activation** — Zhang ... Liang, JCI Insight 2026. https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.199771
   - MLL-AF9 model in Lxn-KO recipients: better survival, fewer LSCs. Reduced LIFR in MSCs → Cxcl9 up → more cytotoxic CD8 T cells; adds benefit to anti-PD-1.
