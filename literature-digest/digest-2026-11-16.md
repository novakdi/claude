# Literature digest — 2026-11-16

Ten papers: CODEX niches and spatial-analysis methods, JMML macrophages and pediatric AML epitypes, stromal suppression of NK cells in MDS, and immune mechanisms of HMA and venetoclax response.

## A lymphoid-enriched spatial immune niche defines an immunotherapy-responsive state in hepatocellular carcinoma
*Cancers · Oct 2026* · Original research · relevance 3/5 · PMID 43026764 · https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers18213442

Integration of scRNA-seq (100 samples), spatial transcriptomics, 51-plex CODEX and bulk cohorts in hepatocellular carcinoma treated with atezolizumab plus bevacizumab.

- Nine spatial niches were defined; Niche 9 is lymphoid-rich (B, plasma, NK, T cells, macrophages, TLS-like features).
- Niche 9 activity is enriched in immunotherapy responders, and co-registered CODEX confirms its immune composition at the protein level.
- A nine-gene Niche 9 signature predicted response and better outcome in independent cohorts.

**Why it matters:** A worked example of using CODEX to validate spatial niches and link them to therapy response.

## Cellular proximity and conditional coordination in the colorectal and pancreatic tumor microenvironment: a patient-level analysis of multiplexed imaging
*bioRxiv · Oct 2026* · Preprint · methods · relevance 3/5 · PMID 42999117 · https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.08.757632

Statistical framework separating proximity, recurrent neighborhoods and conditional co-variation of cell types, applied to 140 CODEX images (35 colorectal cancer patients) and 207 multiplex IF images (pancreas).

- Proximity and conditional association can disagree, because cell-dense regions inflate all types and images from one patient are not independent.
- In colorectal cancer, epithelial–myeloid conditional association differed between inflammatory subtypes with no proximity difference.
- Recurrent neighborhoods best predicted patient groups (AUC 0.79); available as the R package spatialMIFtools.

**Why it matters:** Practical guidance for analyzing CODEX data at the patient level, directly relevant to your downstream statistics.

## The deep learning framework Hist2Prot infers single-cell spatial proteomics from histology to predict biomarkers and clinical outcomes
*Nature Cancer · Nov 2026* · Methods · relevance 3/5 · PMID 43026320 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01270-8

Deep learning model trained on ~51 million cells with same-section H&E and G4X spatial proteomics to predict protein profiles from routine histology.

- Predicted single-cell protein profiles match measured ones in internal, same-platform and cross-platform cohorts (~72 million extra cells).
- Virtual proteins preserve immune, stromal and tumor phenotypes and tissue organization.
- Across 1,774 patients in seven cohorts, virtual spatial proteomics improved biomarker, prognosis and immunotherapy-response prediction over H&E alone.

**Why it matters:** Solid tumors only, but shows how spatial proteomics could be extended to large archival H&E cohorts for outcome correlation.

## Phagocytosis activity is retained by JMML macrophages and increased by CD47 inhibition
*Br J Haematol · Nov 2026* · Original research · relevance 4/5 · PMID 43001447 · https://doi.org/10.1111/bjh.70871

Human JMML spleens, Mx-Cre;Ptpn11 mutant mice and a patient-derived xenograft used to study clonal macrophages and CD47 in juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia.

- PTPN11-mutant JMML spleens have more M1-like macrophages than KRAS-mutant cases or controls.
- Clonal JMML macrophages stay phagocytic (M1-like most active); CD47 is up on JMML cells and blocking it boosts phagocytosis in vitro.
- In the PDX model, magrolimab lowered leukemia burden in spleen but not bone marrow, with no synergy with azacitidine.

**Why it matters:** Pediatric myeloid neoplasm, tissue macrophage phenotyping plus a mouse model; the spleen-vs-marrow gap points to a protective marrow niche.

## Integrated multi-omics reveals methylation-linked biological heterogeneity in intermediate-risk pediatric acute myeloid leukemia
*iScience · Nov 2026* · Original research · relevance 4/5 · PMID 43022221 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.118028

DNA methylation, whole-exome and transcriptome profiling of intermediate-risk pediatric AML, with projection onto an independent pediatric cohort.

- Methylation clustering defines three epitypes: E1 inflammatory/stress-response, E2 proliferative, E3 developmental with global hypomethylation.
- Different mutations converge on the same pathways within each epitype; immune activation agrees across epigenetic and transcriptomic layers.
- E1 has worse survival than E2 and E3; the methylation programs are partly conserved in the validation cohort.

**Why it matters:** A candidate stratifier for the intermediate-risk group, where outcome correlation of your spatial data may be most informative.

## Advances in CBFA2T3::GLIS2 fusion gene in pediatric acute myeloid leukemia: from molecular mechanisms to targeted therapeutic strategies
*Ann Hematol · Aug 2026* · Review · relevance 3/5 · PMID 43004022 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s00277-026-07227-2

Review of the CBFA2T3::GLIS2 fusion, the most common alteration in non-Down syndrome acute megakaryoblastic leukemia in children.

- The fusion comes from a cryptic chr16 inversion; CBFA2T3 exon 11–GLIS2 exon 3 is the main transcript.
- It is linked to chemoresistance and poor outcome, and also occurs in cytogenetically normal AML subtypes beyond AMKL.
- Covers leukemogenic mechanisms and emerging targeted therapies.

**Why it matters:** Background on a high-risk pediatric AML subtype you are likely to meet in your cohort.

## Mesenchymal stromal cells drive immune escape in MDS and sAML through metabolic rewiring of NK cells
*Leukemia · Nov 2026* · Original research · relevance 5/5 · PMID 43003869 · https://doi.org/10.1038/s41375-026-03172-1

Paired ATAC-seq and RNA-seq of NK cells co-cultured with mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) from MDS and secondary AML patients versus healthy donors.

- MDS/sAML MSCs remodel NK-cell chromatin, closing promoters of genes for stress response and survival.
- Healthy MSCs protect NK cells; diseased MSCs lose this support and instead cause mitochondrial fragmentation and NK apoptosis.
- The net effect is lower NK cytotoxicity against leukemia, suggesting mitochondria-protective strategies to restore immune surveillance.

**Why it matters:** A direct stroma–immune cell interaction in the MDS marrow; MSC–NK proximity is something CODEX can measure in your pediatric samples.

## Modulation of the RNA-sensing RIG/MAVS pathway bypasses resistance to hypomethylating agents in acute myeloid leukemia
*HemaSphere · Nov 2026* · Original research · relevance 4/5 · PMID 43017577 · https://doi.org/10.1002/hem3.70523

Human and murine AML lines, mouse AML models with RIG-I or MAVS knockout, and a small patient series examine how HMAs trigger antileukemic immunity.

- Azacitidine re-activates endogenous retroviruses whose RNA binds RIG-I, driving MAVS signaling and type I interferon release.
- Deleting RIG-I or MAVS in AML cells abolished HMA efficacy in mice by blocking host interferon signaling and T-cell expansion.
- HMA resistance came with ERV silencing; ATRA restored RIG-I signaling and response in resistant AML in mice.

**Why it matters:** Links therapy response to the marrow immune compartment, with a mouse model design relevant to your functional validation aim.

## Two immune states in venetoclax-resistant AML: antigen-presentation silencing and checkpoint exhaustion
*Curr Cancer Drug Targets · Nov 2026* · Original research · relevance 3/5 · PMID 43023442 · https://doi.org/10.2174/0115680096543389261110065041

Computational analysis of 367 BeatAML patients with ex vivo venetoclax response and RNA-seq, validated with TCGA-LAML methylation data.

- Venetoclax resistance splits into an immune-cold state (low antigen presentation, low checkpoints) and a checkpoint-high, T-cell-infiltrated state.
- The cold state shows promoter hypermethylation of MHC class I/II and checkpoint genes, independent of NPM1 status.
- PD-L1 testing alone would misread the cold state; antigen-presentation methylation is proposed as a biomarker. Findings are correlative.

**Why it matters:** Adult cohort and bulk data, but the two immune states are testable hypotheses for spatial immune phenotyping and therapy response.

## AI-assisted single-cell transcriptomics defines immunosuppressive ecosystem states and CD274-centered communication networks in myelodysplastic syndrome
*Front Cell Dev Biol · Oct 2026* · Original research · relevance 2/5 · PMID 43031997 · https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2026.1897026

Reanalysis of public MDS bone marrow scRNA-seq against a healthy reference, with cell-line validation of immune regulatory genes.

- Eleven immune states; MDS shifts T/NK cells toward cytotoxic and exhaustion-like programs.
- CD274 (PD-L1)-high cells carry TGF-β and checkpoint programs, alongside TGFB1, LGALS9–HAVCR2 and IL10 communication axes.
- Cell-line validation is limited and inconsistent; the authors call it hypothesis-generating.

**Why it matters:** Lists candidate MDS immune checkpoints to consider for a CODEX panel; evidence is weak.
