# Literature digest — 2026-10-19

No new pediatric-AML/MDS CODEX papers this cycle (PubMed, entries 2026-09-20 to 2026-10-19). Closest matches below.

## Pediatric AML
**1. Reactive oxygen species define leukemia stem cell identity in pediatric AML** — Cancer Lett 2026 · PMID 42830144 · doi:10.1016/j.canlet.2026.218866
- Sorted ROS-low (RL) vs ROS-high cells from pediatric AML at diagnosis.
- RL cells: quiescent, serial colony formation, mouse engraftment, localize to hypoxic regions in a 3D niche model, cytarabine-resistant → functional LSCs.
- RL cells have stemness/adhesion/niche-interaction programs, low OXPHOS yet depend on complex I.
- RL-derived signature identifies LSCs in pediatric scRNA-seq and is prognostic.

## AML microenvironment / immunity
**2. Immune and cellular determinants of response to HMA-venetoclax in AML** — HemaSphere 2026 · PMID 42871763 · doi:10.1002/hem3.70498
- 132 patients (clinical + genomic), scRNA-seq + TCR in 10.
- Responders: expanded cytotoxic CD8 T cells, higher TCR diversity, antigen-presentation activation.
- Non-responders: inflammatory signaling, impaired myeloid maturation, IDO1+ DC-rich suppressive niche, stem-like/EMT state.
- LAG-3 marks dysfunctional T cells; candidate biomarker/target.

**3. Clonally expanded CD8 T cells in AML adopt an NK-like program** (bioRxiv preprint) · PMID 42856304 · doi:10.64898/2026.09.30.755147
- Mass cytometry of 46 diagnostic AML marrows + scRNA/TCR-seq.
- CD8 T cells skew to TEMRA; classic exhaustion rare; TEMRA are functional (IFNγ/TNFα).
- Clonal expansion in TEMRA; most expanded clones of unknown specificity express NK receptors (LaNKi score), validated in other datasets.

**4. Inflammatory network remodeling in AML is driven by neutrophil-centric self-amplifying circuits** — Int J Mol Sci 2026 · PMID 42883947 · doi:10.3390/ijms27198703
- scRNA-seq (3 AML vs 6 donors) + CellChat; communication shifts from T cells to myeloid/neutrophils (CXCL8–CXCR2, CCL3–CCR1).
- Luminex in 46 AML/26 controls; inflammatory risk score (CXCL8, CCL2, IL-1A), C-index 0.729, splits ELN2022 intermediate risk.
- Caveat: very small scRNA cohort.

## Murine models / niche
**5. Latexin deletion in the BM niche suppresses leukemia via LIFR downregulation and immune activation** — JCI Insight 2026 · PMID 42847991 · doi:10.1172/jci.insight.199771
- MLL-AF9 AML in Lxn-KO recipients: better survival, fewer LSCs.
- Stromal scRNA-seq: reduced LIFR in MSCs → higher Cxcl9 → CD8 T-cell recruitment/cytotoxicity; additive with anti-PD-1.
- Good template for niche-focused murine validation.

## MDS
**6. Targeting TGFBR1 with TP-6379 improves hematopoiesis in low-risk MDS ex vivo** — Cells 2026 · PMID 42782774 · doi:10.3390/cells15181674
- S100A9-driven inflammation + TGFβ in MDS marrow; ALK5-specific TP-6379 restored hematopoiesis in primary low-risk MDS BM-MNCs (esp. spliceosome-mutant), rescued S100A9 effect, validated in S100A9-Tg mice.

## Spatial / multiplexed imaging methods (other contexts)
**7. T cells form distinct spatially restricted immune responses in synovium (PsA vs RA)** — Front Immunol 2026 · PMID 42876702 · doi:10.3389/fimmu.2026.1946638
- High-multiplex IHC in synovial biopsies; neighborhood analysis around lining fibroblasts. PsA: Th1/Th17 closest then Th2/Th9; RA: Th9/Th17/Th22 barrier; MTX ~50% fewer Th cells nearby.
- Relevance: stromal-centered neighborhood analysis and treatment-effect comparison, analogous to niche analysis in marrow.

**8. Single-cell and multi-omics profiling of the multiple myeloma immune niche** (review) — Cancers 2026 · PMID 42882852 · doi:10.3390/cancers18193158
- Reviews spatial transcriptomics/multiplexed imaging of marrow architecture: immune exclusion, stromal support, metabolic niches, and spatial accessibility in CAR-T/T-cell-engager response.
